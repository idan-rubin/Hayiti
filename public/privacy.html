Privacy policy

Who operates Hayiti

Idan Rubin operates Hayiti, a personal trip journal hosted on ChatGPT Sites. This policy covers the public beta.

What you share

Signing in allows Hayiti to use a site-specific user ID to store and retrieve your journal. You choose the trip names, dates, places, coordinates, notes, source labels and references, and optional photos to save. A home base is optional. When using a receipt or calendar entry, share only the itinerary details you want to keep.

Where information goes

OpenAI provides hosting and sign-in. Journal records are stored in the hosted site’s Cloudflare R2 storage. When you use the plugin, the requested trip list or day is returned to your ChatGPT conversation. The day tool returns photo counts. Photos remain in the journal unless you export a backup yourself.

Map and address requests

Your browser requests map tiles for the area you view from OpenStreetMap. If you use address search, it sends the text you enter to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service. Those services receive the network information needed to respond, including your IP address.

Imports, exports and retention

The web app parses imported files in your browser and saves the itinerary details and photos you choose. Signed-in trips stay in your journal until you delete them. Use trip settings to delete a trip and its stops from the active journal. An unsigned web session stays in its tab. A standalone copy may store its journal in the browser. You control downloaded files and copies shared with others. ChatGPT conversation history has its own controls.

Hosted route links

When you create a link, Hayiti stores a snapshot of the stops and route geometry for the day or trip you select. Anyone with that link can view the map. You decide whether to include the starting point. Photos, expenses, private notes and source references are excluded. The snapshot stays available until you revoke its link under Share. Revocation also applies to forwarded copies of that URL. Copies that a viewer already saved separately cannot be recalled.

Contact

For access, correction or deletion questions, contact Hayiti support. Describe the request without sending account numbers, passwords, full receipts or booking codes.
