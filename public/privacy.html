Privacy policy

Who operates Hayiti

Idan Rubin operates Hayiti, a personal trip journal hosted on ChatGPT Sites. This policy covers the public beta.

What you share

Signing in allows Hayiti to use a site-specific user ID to store and retrieve your journal. You choose the trip names, dates, places, coordinates, notes, source labels and references, and optional photos to save. A home base is optional. When using a receipt or calendar entry, share only the itinerary details you want to keep.

Where information goes

OpenAI provides hosting and sign-in. Journal records are stored in the hosted site’s Cloudflare R2 storage. When you use the plugin, the requested trip list or day is returned to your ChatGPT conversation. The day tool returns photo counts. Photos remain in the journal unless you export a backup yourself.

Map and address requests

Your browser requests map tiles for the area you view from OpenStreetMap. When you search for an address, or save a stop or a daily starting point with an address and no map pin, your browser sends that address text to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to find its place on the map. So that nearby results come first, the request also names the region around the trip’s existing pins, an area tens of kilometres wide. To keep an address off that service, set the pin on the map or enter coordinates before saving. Those services receive the network information needed to respond, including your IP address.

Imports, exports and retention

The web app parses imported files in your browser and saves the itinerary details and photos you choose. Signed-in trips stay in your journal until you delete them. Use trip settings to delete a trip and its stops from the active journal. A trip made in the web app without signing in is saved in that browser’s storage on your device and stays there until you delete the trip, clear the site’s data or sign in from that tab, which moves it into your journal. Anyone who later opens Hayiti in the same browser can see that trip, and a person who signs in there is shown it and asked before it is added to their journal. On a shared computer, delete the trip when you finish. A standalone copy may store its journal in the browser. You control downloaded files and copies shared with others. ChatGPT conversation history has its own controls.

Hosted route links

When you create a link, Hayiti stores a snapshot of the stops and route geometry for the day or trip you select. Anyone with that link can view the map. You decide whether to include the starting point. Photos, expenses, private notes and source references are excluded. The snapshot stays available until you revoke its link under Share. Revocation also applies to forwarded copies of that URL. Copies that a viewer already saved separately cannot be recalled.

Contact

For access, correction or deletion questions, email rubin.idan@gmail.com. Describe the request without sending account numbers, passwords, full receipts or booking codes.
