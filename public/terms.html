Terms of use

Using the beta

Hayiti is provided by Idan Rubin for maintaining a personal travel journal. You can create trips, record places you visited and edit their order. Features may change during the beta.

Your content

You retain your rights to the information you add. You authorize Hayiti to process it to save, display, export or share the itinerary as you request. Only add material you may use and share.

Checking a route

Map positions, walking estimates and the order inferred from past visits may be approximate. Review imported information before saving or sharing it. Use current local directions when traveling.

Export and support

You can export your trip from the journal. A file you send to someone else is a separate copy. For help, contact Hayiti support.
